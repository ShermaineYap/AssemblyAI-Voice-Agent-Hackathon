Voice agent · AssemblyAI

Rehearse your viva out loud, with an examiner who read your report.

Paste your abstract or a job description. VivaVoice questions you on it by voice, follows up on what you actually said, scores each answer against a rubric while you talk, and gives you a written report at the end.

Use headphones for the best experience. Audio goes to AssemblyAI for the live session only; nothing is stored by VivaVoice.

1 · Listens

AssemblyAI streaming speech-to-text with your project's terms as keyterms, and turn detection tuned for thinking pauses.

2 · Probes

The examiner builds follow-ups on your exact words and pushes on vague claims, like a real panel.

3 · Scores

Tool calls fill the rubric live on screen, then a report with strengths, fixes and practice questions.
